Description

Tidy Up the Town is a cozy tidy-up game where your apprentice-level magical mishaps have caused the entire contents of the town's market stalls to end up strewn around the main square...

Power up and upgrade your magical abilities to collect and sort hundreds of different items into their correct market stalls and shelves for the merchants, and bring life back to your town!

Key Features

  • 1,000 items across 10 different market stalls including Blacksmith, Apothecary, Scribe, Butcher and more!
  • Full Controller support.
  • Unlock magical Spells and Abilities to speed up your sorting.
  • Obtainable animal companions.
  • No time limits, no game over state. Tidy Up at your own pace.
