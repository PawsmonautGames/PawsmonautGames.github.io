Description

FATHOM is the next game from Pawsmonaut Games — the solo-indie studio behind sleeper survival-crafting hit AETHUS.

FATHOM is a laid-back desktop companion idler game set beneath the waves of an alien ocean. It sits on the bottom of your screen while you work or play.

Deploy diving crews and submersibles to observe and catalogue the life of this strange world, while harvesting resources to expand your floating research base and extend your reach further into the depths.

Use the research you generate to unlock new scientific tools and minigames as well as new constructions for your platform.
Travel to further regions to discover new species and make new discoveries, while unravelling a mystery that involves the very planet itself.

Set on a vast ocean planet in the AETHUS universe, FATHOM continues the Resource Worlds Saga begun in AETHUS from a new perspective.

FATHOM is coming to PC via Steam in 2027, co-published by Pawsmonaut Games and Exothermic. Wishlist it on Steam.
If you're press and would like an early look, please feel free to reach out at hello@pawsmonaut.games.

Key Features

  • A desktop companion that sits at the bottom of your screen while you work or play.
  • Automate expeditions with your platform crew and/or play mini-games to maximise productivity.
  • Upgrade and expand your research platform to unlock new biomes and features.
  • Uncover the mystery of this ocean planet and the corporate machinations that have brought you here.
  • Distinct explorable biomes like volcanic smokestacks, cyan reefs and alien crystal caves.
  • Beautiful human-made pixel-art marine life, from tiny fish to massive creatures.
  • An extensive research tree for unlocking new features and research tools, submersibles, bonuses and more.
  • A unique prestige system that provides powerful bonuses to gameplay as you prestige.
  • New discoveries, lore and stories that expand the deep AETHUS universe.
