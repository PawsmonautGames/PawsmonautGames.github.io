How it works

Every finished game is shown, so a player can appear more than once. Fewer steps needed ranks higher, and ties are broken by the quicker run. Each season has its own leaderboard.

Taking part is your choice, and you can leave and delete your entries at any time from Settings > Privacy in the game. See the privacy policy for exactly what is sent and kept.

Runs that look impossible are removed at the discretion of the developer.
